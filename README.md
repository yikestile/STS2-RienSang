# STS2-RienSang

## Description

The Index Nursefather Yi Sang character mod from Limbus Company.

<img width="1920" height="1080" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9f6529f2-9c2a-415e-9c93-77171d34dcbc" />


## Main features

- The Prescript: Receive a new Prescript every turn. Fulfill them to be rewarded, failing to do so will result in punishment.
- Procuration [Hermes]: Special Cards are rewarded upon reaching max stacks.
- Current Content: 75 cards, 21 powers, and 4 unique relics.
- Attacks using [Caduceus] and E.G.O cards.

## Oracle Device - [Caduceus]
*A random weapon is assigned every time Caduceus is used. Each weapon grants a unique tactical effect.*

<img width="25" height="25" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6645c350-e434-48f1-813e-0ac3a68b3473" /> **Hatchet:** Deals `Blunt` damage. Gain `2 Poise Potency`.

<img width="25" height="25" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2bce1400-1cd7-4324-b2ae-69286d565dec" /> **Stiletto:** Deals `Pierce` damage. Inflict `2 Sinking Potency`.
  
<img width="25" height="25" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7059a8b5-094c-4625-8d7c-3442f6bb2025" /> **Bastard Sword:** Deals `Slash` damage (+5%). Gain `1 Strength` next turn (Max 2/turn).

<img width="25" height="25" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d27f5281-f071-44c9-9f91-f2df61f3f041" /> **Rapier:** Deals `Pierce` damage (+5%). Inflicts `1 Weak` next turn (Max 2/turn).

<img width="25" height="25" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7ab46578-e9f8-4b30-accc-d51d5ea6ea2a" /> **Hammer:** Deals `Blunt` damage (+5%). **Removes Blocks** from the target.

<img width="25" height="25" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c28da229-2d15-4c0c-bc72-863ccde18a4f" /> **Greatsword:** Deals `Slash` damage (+15%). Inflict `1 Slash Fragility` (Max 2/turn).

<img width="25" height="25" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/151bef86-6631-4a09-b478-b12d7bfa8416" /> **Lance:** Deals `Pierce` damage (+15%). Inflict `1 Pierce Fragility` (Max 2/turn).

<img width="25" height="25" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b9cc1f25-abac-400c-9e08-edd731c4ecc5" /> **Whip:** Deals `Blunt` damage (+15%). Inflict `1 Blunt Fragility` (Max 2/turn).

<img width="25" height="25" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7ed401b8-8397-4fd6-9148-3311b9a6b6b4" /> **Scythe:** Deals `Slash` damage (+30%). **Guarantees Critical Hit**.


### For Slash/Pierce/Blunt Rate UP:
- The chance is: (75 - (Karmic Consequence / 2))% chance to produce a Slash/Pierce/Blunt weapon
